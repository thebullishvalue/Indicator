## Hypotheses

- **TSMOM** (round 1): Reference: multi-horizon (1/3/6/12 m) volatility-normalised momentum of the close, continuous. The engine to beat.
- **BOUND** (round 1): A trail is binary. Does sign(S), the side of TSMOM's decision boundary, keep TSMOM's edge? Literature: continuous sizing beats sign (Baltas & Kosowski 2013).
- **TRAIL126** (round 1): The acceptance trail failed at 20 bars, where timing skill is nil. Re-horizoned to a 126-bar (6-month) profile, where trend premia live, it may trend. Spec v2 otherwise.
- **SKIP21** (round 1): Single stocks reverse at 1 month (Jegadeesh 1990). Dropping the 21-bar horizon (63/126/252 only) should lift stocks without hurting other classes.
- **PMOM** (round 1): Pragati's premise: moves made on high participation carry more information. Weight each return by the conviction engine's participation w.
- **TRAIL252** (round 2): The horizon lever continued: the acceptance trail on a 12-month (252-bar) profile.
- **TRAILX** (round 2): Horizon diversification as TSMOM does it: the mean of the acceptance trail's state at 63, 126 and 252 bars.
- **TRAILX+TS** (round 2): Two definitions of trend, where value is accepted (TRAILX) and where price has gone (TSMOM), each in [-1, 1]; if they err at different times their mean is steadier.
- **TS+T20** (round 2): Momentum crashes at turning points (Daniel & Moskowitz 2016; Garg et al. 2021): halve TSMOM while Pragati's 20-bar trail points against it.
- **T126+TS** (round 3): The trail gives the side, momentum the size: (TRAIL126 + S)/2, so the sign is the 6-month trail's and TSMOM sets the size from ~0 to 1.
- **T63126+TS** (round 3): Horizon diversification without the failed 12-month leg: (mean(TRAIL63, TRAIL126) + S)/2.
- **T126xTS** (round 3): Consensus only: TRAIL126's side sized max(0, S*side); flat when the two definitions of trend disagree.
